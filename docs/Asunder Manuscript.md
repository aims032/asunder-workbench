## Spine

*Working premise, Oct 17.* The Asunder is a place without entropy — no arrow of time, nothing is ever lost. The Physicist reaches into it for the person he lost. Every extraction, every transmission, imports the arrow of time into her: a past, a future, and so mortality.

## People

- **The Physicist** — begins wanting to use the place as a resource (something energy-like, not a mineral); ends wanting to protect her, even die for her.
- **Asunder** — the person and the place. Why she was manifested, and for what, is unknown.
